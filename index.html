What is a recoverable gift?

A normal BCH payment cannot be undone. If you send it to the wrong address, or to someone who never picks it up, it is gone. A recoverable gift locks the coins in a contract with two ways out: the recipient can claim at any time, and the sender can recover once a waiting period has passed.

  • Role 1

    Sender

    Funds the gift and chooses the recovery delay. Cannot recover early, and cannot block the recipient.

  • Role 2

    Recipient

    Claims to a payout address of their choice with their own key. No waiting period applies to the recipient.

  • Role 3

    Recovery

    A separate recovery key held by the sender. It signs the return of the funds, only after the delay has matured.

Two keys, three steps

  1. Fund. Sender and recipient exchange public keys, agree on the delay, and the sender funds one contract output.
  2. Claim. The recipient signs a claim to their chosen payout address.
  3. Recover. After the delay, the sender can sign a recovery. The first confirmed spend wins.

The delay is a minimum age in blocks, not an exact deadline. The recipient can still claim after recovery becomes available, until a recovery confirms.
